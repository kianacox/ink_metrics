What we're doing and why

No waitlist
Site is live. Using founding member pricing (first 50 at £10.99) with a real counter to create honest urgency instead.
No Reddit
Tattoo artists don't discuss finances publicly there, and the channel is saturated with low-quality content right now. Not worth the effort.
MTD deferred
Data download functionality isn't ready yet. Won't push MTD compliance angle until features are in place and claims are fully verified. Avoids over-promising.
Core message focus
Lead with financial tracking, auto-applied maths, and client relationship management. These are live, provable, and genuinely differentiated.
Faceless content
Neither founder nor partner are comfortable on camera. All Reels and TikToks built from screen recordings, static text, slideshow founder notes, and hands-at-work clips. Aligns with where the algorithm is heading — faceless B2B-ish content currently outperforms talking-head content in this niche.
Founding counter
Show real signup count on pricing page. Seed honestly: founder + partner + close contacts before going public. No inflated numbers.
Messaging

The three angles that will convert

Every piece of content — organic, paid, or community — should map to one of these. Rotate between them to avoid repetition while staying on-brand.

Pillar 1 · Pain
"Do you actually know what you took home last month?"
Targets the gut feeling most artists have — that their bookings look great but the money doesn't add up. No solution mentioned yet. Just the question.
Pillar 2 · Aspiration
"Log a session in 30 seconds. Know your true profit instantly."
Shows what clarity feels like. Use real dashboard screenshots from your partner's data. Authenticity over polish — this audience spots mockups immediately.
Pillar 3 · Trust
"Built by a developer whose partner is a working tattoo artist."
Your strongest long-term differentiator. Delivered through build-in-public posts, hands-at-work clips from your partner's station, and slideshow-format founder notes — never on-camera. The genuineness comes from specificity, not from showing faces.

What to avoid: Don't lead with features (dashboard, analytics, export). Lead with the outcome — "know your real take-home." Features are proof points, not hooks.

What's not in scope yet

Deferred until data export is built and all compliance claims are verified. Don't market what you can't fully deliver yet.

Worth building from month 2 onwards — a definitive "tattoo artist finance UK" guide will compound over time. Not a pre-launch priority.

Search intent for this category is too low-volume to be efficient at £200/mo. Revisit once organic SEO data shows which terms actually convert.

High impact but requires time and travel. Revisit at 6 months once there's traction to point to and a story to tell in person.
